🍎 Healthy Eating

Balanced meals with low glycemic index foods help maintain stable blood sugar levels.

  • ✓ Eat regular meals
  • ✓ Choose whole grains
  • ✓ Include vegetables

🏃 Physical Activity

Regular exercise helps control blood sugar and improves overall health.

  • ✓ 30 min daily walking
  • ✓ Check sugar before exercise
  • ✓ Stay hydrated

💊 Medication Management

Take medications as prescribed and never skip doses without consulting your doctor.

  • ✓ Set reminders
  • ✓ Track side effects
  • ✓ Refill on time

🚨 Emergency Signs

Know when to seek immediate medical help.

  • ⚡ Extreme thirst
  • ⚡ Frequent urination
  • ⚡ Dizziness or confusion
